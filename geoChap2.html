Chapter 2: Location and Extent

What is Location and Extent?

The location of a place refers to its specific position on the Earth's surface, while the extent refers to the area it covers. Understanding location and extent helps in recognizing the significance of a place geographically, including its boundaries, climate, and resources.

Understanding Location

Location can be described in terms of geographical coordinates such as latitude and longitude. These coordinates help pinpoint a place on the map or globe. The Earth's surface is divided by lines of latitude (horizontal lines) and longitude (vertical lines). The exact location of a place can be determined by its distance from the Equator and the Prime Meridian.

Understanding Extent

Extent refers to the total area a place or region occupies. The extent of a country or region includes its geographical boundaries, such as mountains, rivers, and seas, which define the region's physical limits.

Geographical Extent of India

India is a vast country with diverse geographical features. It stretches from the snowy mountains in the north to the warm oceans in the south.
